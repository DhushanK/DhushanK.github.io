iBioMed design projects at McMaster. Each one started from a client and ended as hardware.

Osto-Lock

A two-piece ostomy system that stays on when the wearer bends and twists. The barrier clips with a spring, magnets and guides hold the latch, and the pouch closes with a lock-and-roll.

Active living made secure.
